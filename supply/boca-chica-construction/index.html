Boca Chica Construction

Location: Brownsville, TX (South Texas Division) Phone: (956) 443-8004 Visit website →

Business & Scale

Founded in 2019, Boca Chica Construction began as a family-run contractor focused on residential excavation and demolition, and has since grown into a full-scope civil contractor serving industrial, municipal, and federal projects across the Rio Grande Valley. Based near Starbase in Brownsville, the company is positioned to support the site development needs of the region’s fast-growing port, LNG, refining, shipbuilding, aerospace, and manufacturing sectors.

  • Industrial excavation and site grading
  • Selective and structural demolition
  • Underground utility infrastructure (drainage, culvert installation)
  • Contractor support and operated equipment services

The company operates a South Texas division in Brownsville and a Central Florida division in Ocala, and is licensed and insured.

Additional Notes

Boca Chica Construction has received multiple positive reviews on third-party platforms such as HomeAdvisor, with customers noting professional, efficient work and thorough site cleanup.
